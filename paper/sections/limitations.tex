\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Tasks, budget and scale.} The pools come from two multiple-choice task families (logical
deduction, tracking shuffled objects) and one proposal procedure; a closed answer set favours a
stable read-off, which may be larger for free-form answers. At the validity budget of $384$ tokens,
incumbent samples are truncated on $46\%$ (LD7-L), $97\%$ (LD7-L$^\ast$) and $100\%$ (LD7-Q), and the
incumbent's greedy held-out reads on TS7-Q on $99.5\%$ (Table~\ref{tab:reroll}), so these read-off
terms, divergences and that target concern unfinished reasoning; sample truncation was not measured
on the other six pools. On pools with little truncation (LD3-L $0.3\%$, TS7-L $14\%$) the objective
is negative as well ($-0.41$, $-0.66$). Models have 8--9B parameters and ran on one or two shared
48\,GB GPUs: each pool is one proposal draw, Gemma-2-9B contributes one pool, the re-roll study
covers two tasks and two models, and the verification analyses replay a single selection round,
missing how selection errors compound, and set the acceptance margin from each pool's flip rate over
all of its reads rather than within the budget. The calculus and the likelihood ratios need white-box access; the
calculus was verified on four rotary-embedding architectures (insertions and combined edits on
three), the likelihoods against a direct per-token computation.

\paragraph{Engine and review.} The re-roll null is specific to one deterministic engine
configuration and one perturbation (request sizes of $16$ to $100$ questions); batch-invariant
kernels \citep{he2025nondeterminism} would give a different, possibly degenerate, null, so token
edits are shown to move accuracy as much as this engine's numerics, not to be inert. The targets of
Table~\ref{tab:validity} were read by a Hugging Face batched reader of unmeasured reproducibility,
and the importance-sampling reads by an earlier engine configuration whose re-reads were not always
token-identical (Appendix~\ref{app:reader}); \todo{block re-reads of these targets under the
deterministic engine, with re-rolls and truncation}. The later analyses were reviewed only by
fresh-context instances of the model family that ran the research, which cannot catch errors this
family makes systematically.
