\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Models and data.} Task and optimizer model coincide: Llama-3-8B-Instruct and
Gemma-2-9B-it (\greater{}'s models) and Qwen3-8B in non-thinking mode. Benchmarks are
\greater{}'s 21 BIG-Bench Hard tasks \citep{suzgun2023bbh} (50 train, 100 dev, up to 100 test),
GSM8K \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} and FOLIO \citep{han2024folio}, from the \greater{} release with a
fixed-seed split; $41\%$ of our BBH test questions were among those \greater{} used to produce its
published prompts, so comparisons with those prompts are also reported on the clean remainder.
Hypotheses, protocols and every amendment were dated before the data they concern
(Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}); failed hypotheses are reported as registered.

\subsection{Reweighting the model's own samples does not rescue the signal}
\label{sec:exp-validity}
On the eleven pools of \S\ref{sec:diagnosis} (same questions, edits and held-out targets) we drew
$S=16$ reasonings per training question under the incumbent ($\tau=0.7$, no truncation of the
distribution) and computed exact tempered likelihood ratios under every candidate. The kill
criterion was fixed before any of this data existed: the distributional score must beat a random
shortlist in pooled best-3 regret (bootstrap $P\ge0.9$, and in at least $7$ of $11$ pools) and not
fall more than $0.05$ below fresh accuracy on $8$ questions in pooled Spearman. \todo{H-dist
