# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors should communicate constructively, welcome good-faith questions,
focus criticism on work rather than people, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's
private information. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the security
contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed in
good faith with confidentiality and proportionality.
